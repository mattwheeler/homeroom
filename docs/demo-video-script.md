# Homeroom demo video — final script and shot list

Target length: **2:35–2:50**. Devpost requires a public YouTube video under three minutes with voiceover covering the product, Codex, and GPT-5.6.

## Recording rules

- Record at 1920×1080 and 100% browser zoom.
- Use the deployed Worker, not localhost.
- Do not show the private judge code, browser password manager, Cloudflare secrets, Google tokens, or real student data.
- Keep the pointer still unless it is showing the next action.
- Prefer one clean take per section; edit out OAuth waits and typing pauses.

## 0:00–0:18 — The problem

**Visual:** Homeroom root, then enter Emily's student view before the voiceover reaches “Today.”

**Voiceover:**

> School portals tell a student what exists. They rarely help the student decide what to do next. Homeroom is a guardian-connected AI workspace that turns assignments, calendars, school events, and family logistics into one calm next step—without taking control away from the student.

## 0:18–0:50 — Live student context

**Visual:** Emily's Today page. Briefly show the private check-in and the source-updated indicator, then start the recommended assignment.

**Voiceover:**

> This is Emily, a fictional fourteen-year-old preparing for ninth grade and band camp. Her Today view is built from seven read-only Google Classroom classes, coursework, BAND calendar events, district dates, and an official supply list. She can talk through how she is arriving, choose another task, or start the recommended first step. The AI can coach and propose; it cannot invent a deadline or change a school source.

## 0:50–1:22 — Task Room and executive-function support

**Visual:** Task Room. Show task-specific steps, change the timebox, complete one step, end the focus session, and return to recent work.

**Voiceover:**

> In the Task Room, Homeroom turns the real assignment into visible, task-specific steps. The time plan adjusts to Emily's focus window and teaches organization, time management, and prioritization without doing the schoolwork for her. Her Homeroom progress is saved, while the original Classroom assignment remains unchanged. When she returns, she can see what she completed and re-enter without shame.

## 1:22–1:45 — Learning, calendars, and official sources

**Visual:** Quick cuts: Calendar category colors, Classes, source-backed Supplies, then one Learning Room.

**Voiceover:**

> Calendar separates schoolwork, band, and district events. Classes stay tied to their live source. Supplies display only items found on the guardian-approved official page. Learning Rooms offer short, visual, class-scoped practice with hints rather than answer generation.

## 1:45–2:12 — Student-controlled guardian loop

**Visual:** Emily previews and sends a guardian-help note. Switch to Matt. Show the Family inbox, acknowledge the note, and show coarse progress.

**Voiceover:**

> When Homeroom notices a parent-only action, Emily decides whether to ask for help. She sees the exact message first. Matt receives only what Emily approved, plus privacy-safe progress such as a completed focus block—never her private coaching conversation or answers. He can acknowledge the note and manage age, learning, safety, link, and source controls.

## 2:12–2:38 — OpenAI and Codex implementation

**Visual:** Keep the product visible. Optionally overlay brief captions: “GPT-5.6 Responses API,” “explicit approval,” “D1 evidence,” and “430 tests.”

**Voiceover:**

> GPT-5.6 Sol powers bounded coaching and live plan proposals through the OpenAI Responses API. Deterministic application code controls identity, permissions, grading, clocks, and every write. Consequential actions require explicit, version-bound approval. Codex was our engineering collaborator from the Golden Experience Contract through the state machine, source adapters, safety architecture, UX iterations, migrations, and release verification—now 430 passing tests plus desktop and mobile end-to-end journeys.

## 2:38–2:52 — Close

**Visual:** Return to Emily's calm Today view, then Homeroom logo.

**Voiceover:**

> Homeroom does not replace teachers or parents. It gives students a trustworthy layer for understanding what is happening, choosing what comes next, learning how to begin, and asking for help while keeping their agency.

## Final edit checklist

- [ ] Total duration is below 3:00.
- [ ] Voiceover explicitly says **Codex** and **GPT-5.6**.
- [ ] The product is visibly working; no static slide dominates the video.
- [ ] The student and guardian loop is both shown and explained.
- [ ] No secret, real minor data, OAuth token, or private calendar URL appears.
- [ ] Upload is public on YouTube and the link works in an incognito browser.
